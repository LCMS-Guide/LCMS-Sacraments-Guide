# Baptism and the Lord's Supper

A searchable guide to Lutheran (LCMS) teaching on Baptism, the Lord's Supper, and the Crux Theologorum (election: why some are saved and not others), in the words of the Lutheran Confessions (*Concordia*, 2nd ed.) and the LCMS, with Bible passages in seven translations and notes on the Greek and Hebrew.

**Read it here:** https://lcms-guide.github.io/LCMS-Sacraments-Guide/

Copyright notices for each Bible translation and for *Concordia* are at the bottom of the page.

**Use it offline:** on a phone, open the link and choose *Add to Home Screen* (iPhone, in Safari) or *Install app* (Android, in a regular Chrome tab; the option doesn't appear in Incognito). On a computer, use the *Download the guide* button in the page's "Use it offline" section.

**Privacy:** the website counts visits anonymously with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), which uses no cookies and collects no personal information. Downloaded copies count nothing.
